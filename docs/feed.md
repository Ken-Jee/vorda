# Saved public updates

The dashboard Feed shows the CISA KEV catalog already fetched during scans.
It reads cached public records only, sorted by date added, up to 100 items.
Each item keeps its publication date, source URL, fetch time, and stale flag.
Missing or bad cache data shows up explicitly in the saved `feed` result.
Coverage is CISA KEV only, not all security news.

Records without a matching saved finding stay general updates.
Local matches link to your selected saved findings without uploading your inventory.
Your action and repair state stays in the Inbox/mailbox.
Being in the Feed does not prove an advisory affects your software.
No account, AI, paid source, or extra background fetch is needed.
Online scans still make their documented advisory requests.
